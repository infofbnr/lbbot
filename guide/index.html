How to Get Rich

A complete, no-BS guide to mastering the LB Bot economy. Built for players who want long-term wealth — not lucky wins.

💡 Core Philosophy

🚫 Gambling Is a Trap

Blackjack, roulette, and slots are designed for fun — not profit. You might win sometimes, but the odds always catch up.

📈 Passive Income Wins

Items generate money over time. This is how wealth compounds even when you’re not actively using commands.

🧰 Economy Tools You Should Use

💼 Items & Shop

Items are the backbone of the economy. Buying the right items early and upgrading them consistently is how rich players stay rich.

⬆️ Upgrades

Upgrades multiply item income. Upgrading a good item is almost always better than buying a weak new one.

⏱ Collect

Uncollected money is wasted money. The faster you collect, the faster you can reinvest and compound.

🏦 Loans (Advanced)

Loans can accelerate growth if used correctly — but reckless borrowing will slow you down.

🧭 Progression Stages

🟢 Early Game — Foundation

  • Use !work and !daily consistently
  • Buy your first income-generating items ASAP
  • Avoid gambling entirely
  • Collect and reinvest every chance you get

🟡 Mid Game — Compounding

  • Upgrade strong items instead of buying random ones
  • Passive income becomes your main money source
  • Reinvest profits immediately
  • Loans are optional, but only with a plan

🔵 Late Game — Scaling

  • Max upgrades on your highest-earning items
  • Passive income outperforms all active commands
  • Gambling is for fun only, not strategy
  • Optimize collection timing and efficiency

🔁 The Winning Loop

Every rich player follows the same cycle. If you break this loop, your progress slows. If you repeat it consistently, wealth compounds fast.
